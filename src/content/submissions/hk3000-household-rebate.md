title: "A HK$3,000 Rebate for Lower-Income Households"
submittedTo: "the Financial Secretary's Office, as part of the 2026–27 Budget consultation"
date: "winter 2025–26"
summary: "Argued for a one-off HK$3,000 rebate aimed at lower-income households. In its favour: poorer families spend almost everything they receive, so each dollar recirculates through local shops, a larger multiplier than the same sum given to higher earners, who save more of it. Against it: the cost to the budget, a risk of mild inflation, and the fact that a one-off payment does nothing about why households are poor. We concluded the benefits outweigh the costs, because the money does the most work per dollar exactly where it is targeted. The 2026–27 Budget later included a one-off tax reduction of up to HK$3,000 per taxpayer."
