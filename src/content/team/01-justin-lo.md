Justin believes economics is one of the most powerful tools we have for
changing lives, if someone is willing to carry it out of the classroom and into
the places where decisions are made. That belief led him to found AI For Good in
June 2023. He started with a simple question: what if students didn't wait to
be old enough to shape policy, and started now? From that question grew a
student-led think tank that puts recommendations in front of the Hong Kong
government every year. It also brings education to children who are too often
overlooked.

Justin's focus is developmental and financial economics: why some communities
are left behind, and what it takes to help them rise. He wants to become an
economist and policymaker whose research turns into action, and whose evidence
reaches people's lives, not just journals. He isn't aiming to understand the
world from a distance. He wants to leave it measurably better than he found it,
and AFG is where he has begun.
