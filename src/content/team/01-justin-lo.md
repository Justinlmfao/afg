Justin believes economics is one of the most powerful tools we have for
changing lives, if someone is willing to carry it out of the classroom and into
the places where decisions are made. That belief led him to found AI For Good in
June 2023. He started with a simple question: what if students didn't wait to
be old enough to shape policy, and started now? From that question grew a
student-led think tank that puts recommendations in front of the Hong Kong
government every year, and a tutoring programme that brings education to
children who are too often overlooked.

Justin's focus is developmental and financial economics: why some communities
are left behind, and what it takes to help them rise. He is drawn to the
questions that sit between a line in a budget and a family's everyday life: who
pays for a policy, who benefits from it, and who is never asked. Leading AFG
has taught him that the answer rarely lies in the data alone. It lies in
carrying that data to the people who can act on it, and in listening to the
people it describes.

He wants to become an economist and policymaker whose research turns into
action, and whose evidence reaches people's lives, not just journals. He isn't
aiming to understand the world from a distance. He wants to leave it measurably
better than he found it, first in Hong Kong and then wherever the same gaps
appear, and AFG is where he has begun.
