Ka Hei co-founded AI For Good because he believes knowledge is most valuable
when it is made understandable and useful to others. He could see how quickly
AI was arriving in classrooms, and how easily it could become one more thing
some students grow up fluent in while others learn to find it distant or
intimidating. Helping build AFG was his way of making sure that gap did not
widen on his watch.

As Vice President, Ka Hei helps shape AI For Good's direction by turning ideas
into projects that students can carry out and communities can benefit from. In
particular, he is interested in helping younger students engage with AI
confidently and responsibly, rather than seeing it as something distant or
difficult to understand. For him, a project succeeds when a child leaves the
room able to use what they have learned, not just remember it.

This belief also connects with Ka Hei's wider interest in healthcare and
rehabilitation, where communication and practical application are just as
important as scientific knowledge itself. A treatment helps no one if the
patient cannot understand it, and a technology changes nothing if people are
afraid to use it. He hopes to pursue a future where science, technology and
human-centred care come together to improve people's lives in meaningful and
accessible ways, and to help build a world where recovery, like learning, is
within everyone's reach.
