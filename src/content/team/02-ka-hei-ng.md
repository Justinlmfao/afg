Ka Hei co-founded AFG believing that the future of healthcare will be decided
by policy as much as by medicine. Behind every waiting list, every unaffordable
treatment and every gap in care, she sees a decision that could be made
differently. She is determined to be part of making it.

As Vice President, Ka Hei helps lead AFG's direction and drives its work on
healthcare policy. She researches how systems can serve patients better and
advocates for reforms that put people first. Her ambition is a Hong Kong, and
eventually a world, where access to good care depends on need rather than
circumstance.
