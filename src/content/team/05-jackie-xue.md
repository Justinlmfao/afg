Jackie is driven by the belief that the people closest to a problem deserve a
voice in solving it. Through the social sciences, he studies how policy shapes
everyday lives. Through advocacy, he works to make sure those lives shape policy
in return.

At AFG, Jackie researches issues, speaks with stakeholders and helps draft
proposals that weigh every side before making the case for change. He believes
the strongest proposals begin with listening: to the families a policy affects,
the teachers who carry it out, and the people who disagree with it. Only once
every side has been heard, he argues, can a recommendation claim to speak for
anyone.

He sees advocacy as a form of power that anyone can claim, and he intends to use
his to make his community fairer and more hopeful. In the long run, he hopes to
help build public institutions that listen before they act, so that the people
closest to a problem are never again the last to be asked how to solve it. For
Jackie, changing the world starts with making sure everyone in it has a say.
