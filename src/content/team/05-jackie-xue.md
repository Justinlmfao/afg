Jackie is driven by the belief that the people closest to a problem deserve a
voice in solving it. Through the social sciences, she studies how policy shapes
everyday lives. Through advocacy, she works to make sure those lives shape
policy in return.

At AFG, Jackie researches issues, speaks with stakeholders and helps draft
proposals that weigh every side before making the case for change. She sees
advocacy as a form of power that anyone can claim, and she intends to use hers
to make her community fairer and more hopeful.
