David lives in the world of mathematics and physics, but he knows numbers alone
don't change minds. As he puts it, "the most powerful weapon is the mastery of
language." Science reveals what is true; language persuades the world to act on
it. David believes that bringing the two together is how the next generation
will solve problems that once seemed impossible.

As AFG's Technical lead, David brings quantitative rigour to the team's research
and builds the tools and platforms that carry its ideas further. In practice,
that means testing whether the numbers in a proposal hold up, whether the
reasoning behind them is sound, and whether the case they make would survive a
sceptical reader.

His aim is to make sure that when AFG speaks, it speaks with evidence, and that
the evidence reaches the people it can help. Beyond AFG, he hopes to work where
science meets society: using mathematics and physics to take on the hardest
problems facing the world, and language to make sure the solutions are
understood, trusted and put to use. He believes the breakthroughs that change
the world will come from people who can both find the answer and explain why it
matters.
