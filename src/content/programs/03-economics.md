Students use AI to understand the economy of their own city. They look at
real Hong Kong data on housing costs, wages and inequality, with AI helping
them search it and turn it into charts. Ideas like supply and demand,
opportunity cost and the wealth gap become questions about their own
neighbourhood. They also run simple AI-assisted simulations to see how prices
and incentives affect each other.
