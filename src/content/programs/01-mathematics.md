We use AI to make abstract maths something children can see and poke at.
Students build a simple model that fits a line to some data, so slope, gradient
and optimisation stop being words in a textbook. They use an AI tutor for
step-by-step explanations at their own level, the kind of one-to-one help that
usually costs money. We also teach them to check the AI's working, and catching
its mistakes turns out to be a great way to learn the maths.
