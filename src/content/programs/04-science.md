Students use AI simulations to run experiments their schools can't afford the
equipment for, like projectile motion, circuits and chemical reactions. They
try image-recognition tools to see how machines "see" the world. They also
learn where AI models get things wrong and where simulations stop matching
reality, which is real scientific thinking.
